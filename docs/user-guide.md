# Run a meet

## Before the meet

1. Create the contest with the wizard. Check the rulebook: lifts, divisions, classes, plates.
2. Enter the entries. Weigh in each lifter on the **Zgłoszenia** tab. Age class, weight class and coefficients update as you type.
3. Declare opening weights. The lifting order exists as soon as lifters have opening weights.
4. Download a backup (**Kopia** tab).
5. Open the stage screen, announcer view and scoreboard on their devices, using the links at the top of the desk. Open each link once while the internet works.

## During the meet (desk)

- The **Stół** tab shows the current attempt and the queue.
- Press **G** for a good lift and **N** for a no lift, or click the buttons. The next attempt becomes current at once.
- After a verdict, the lifter's next attempt appears under "next attempt". Type the weight and press Enter. The desk checks the weight against the bar, clamps and plates and suggests the nearest weight that can be loaded.
- Move a lifter with the Earlier and Later buttons. "Make current" sets any attempt as current. **Cofnij ostatnią zmianę** undoes the last change.
- Any past attempt can be corrected in the **Boje** tab.

## When the internet drops

Keep working. The bar at the top shows "no connection" and how many changes wait to be sent. Screens on the same Wi-Fi keep updating. When the connection returns, the changes are sent in order. A change that no longer fits is listed for you to check.

## Scoreboard

On the desk, choose a filter and press **Pokaż na tablicy**. The TV changes within a second. **Pokaż kod QR** shows the public results address on the TV.

## After the meet

The **Wyniki** tab lists every classification. Each can be exported as a federation CSV, PDF or JPG. The public results page shows the same tables and updates live.
